# Code of Conduct

AppPilot contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces
welcoming, professional, and focused on constructive collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Give specific, technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not publish device, signing, authentication, or App
  data that is not yours to disclose.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `tristan_yeung@outlook.com`. Reports will
be reviewed confidentially and handled proportionately. Maintainers may edit,
remove, reject, or ban contributions and participants whose behavior violates
this policy.
